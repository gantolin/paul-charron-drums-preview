Free Online Metronome

A free online metronome for drum practice. Set the tempo, pick the beats in a bar, and press start. It runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer.

100 beats per minute Andante

How to use the metronome

  • Set the tempoDrag the slider, use the plus and minus buttons, or tap the "Tap tempo" button in time with a song.
  • Pick the barChoose 2 to 7 beats in a bar. The first beat sounds higher so you can hear where the bar starts.
  • Split the beatChoose eighth notes, triplets or sixteenth notes to hear the clicks between the beats.
  • Speed up as you playTick the box and the tempo climbs by itself every few bars.
  • KeyboardSpace starts and stops. The arrow keys change the tempo by one.
