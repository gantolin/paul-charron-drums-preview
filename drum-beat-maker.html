Free Online Drum Beat Maker

A free online drum beat maker. Tap the squares to build a beat with six drum sounds, pick a starting groove, and press play. It runs in your browser.

Each row is a drum and each square is a sixteenth note. The brighter squares are the beats: 1, 2, 3, 4. Tap a drum's name to hear it.

How to use the beat maker

  • Start with a groovePick Rock, Funk, Boom bap or another starting groove, then change it.
  • Add and remove hitsTap any square. Kick is orange, snare and tom are green, cymbals are gold.
  • Change the feelSpeed sets the tempo. Swing pushes every second sixteenth note late, for a looser feel.
  • Keep it"Copy a link to this beat" saves the beat in a link you can send or bookmark.
  • KeyboardSpace starts and stops.
