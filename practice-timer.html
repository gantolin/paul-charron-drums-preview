Drum Practice Timer

A drum practice timer that splits a practice session into blocks and chimes when it is time to move on. Pick a length, press start, and play.

Warm-up 4:00 Next: Technique

    Session: 20 minutes

    How to use the practice timer

    • Pick a length15, 20, 30 or 45 minutes. The timer divides it into four blocks.
    • Make it yoursRename any block and change its minutes. Your blocks are remembered on this device.
    • PlayA chime sounds when a block ends and the next one starts. The screen stays on while the timer runs, on phones that allow it.

    The four blocks are a starting point. If your teacher gives you a practice plan, type that in.
