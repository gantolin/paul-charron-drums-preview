Step Up For Students Drum Lessons in Loxahatchee

Step Up For Students drum lessons in Loxahatchee work like any other lesson with Paul Charron. The difference is how the lesson gets paid for. This page explains it for scholarship families.

Which scholarships cover drum lessons

Step Up For Students lists music lessons as an authorized elective. Its Provider Handbook says students on these four scholarships may use funds for elective lessons:

  • Family Empowerment Scholarship for Students with Unique Abilities (FES-UA)
  • Family Empowerment Scholarship for Educational Options (FES-EO)
  • Florida Tax Credit Scholarship (FTC)
  • Personalized Education Program (PEP)

Step Up For Students decides what each student's funds can pay for. Check your scholarship's Purchasing Guide, or ask Step Up at (877) 735-7837, before you count on it.

Two ways to pay for lessons

Pay Paul directly from the scholarship

Find Paul Charron Drums in the Marketplace inside your EMA account. Call or text Paul to set up lessons. After a lesson has taken place, you approve the payment in EMA and Step Up pays Paul.

NEEDS CLIENT: the exact business name on Paul's EMA listing.

Pay Paul, then ask Step Up to reimburse you

You pay for lessons yourself and send a reimbursement request in EMA. Paul gives you an invoice with what Step Up asks for: the student's name, the instructor's name, the type of service, the dates, the rate and how you paid.

Step Up also asks for proof that the instructor qualifies. Paul can give you that paperwork. NEEDS CLIENT: Paul's resume showing his teaching since 2008.

How to start

  1. Call or text Paul at (561) 350-1434 and say your child has a Step Up For Students scholarship.
  2. Book a first lesson at the Loxahatchee studio.
  3. Pay through EMA or ask for an invoice to send in for reimbursement.

NEEDS CLIENT: Paul's lesson price and length. Paul sets his own price. Step Up does not set it.

Questions about Step Up For Students and drum lessons

Is Paul a Step Up For Students provider?

Yes. Paul teaches drum lessons under the Elective Courses category, which covers music lessons. NEEDS CLIENT: confirm the wording once the approval email arrives.

Can a scholarship pay for a drum kit or sticks?

The Provider Handbook says elective funds can cover lessons, equipment and consumables tied to an elective. What your student's scholarship will approve is Step Up's decision, so ask them first.

Where do I learn about the scholarships?

On the Step Up For Students website. Paul teaches the lessons. Step Up For Students runs the scholarships and answers questions about your funds.
